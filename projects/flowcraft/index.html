Question 01 · Purpose

What is the goal of this workflow?

Every good process map starts by naming the outcome and who it serves — before any boxes get drawn. This becomes the top line of your SIPOC scope.

Fuzzy on the details? Most people don't know their own workflow — they know stories about it. I'll ask questions and reconstruct the real process from how it actually happened last time.
Question 02 · Sequence

Walk me through the steps, in order.

For each step: what happens, who owns it, what kind of step it is, whether it's manual, and roughly how long the work takes. Handoffs and manual work are where most waste hides — be honest here.
